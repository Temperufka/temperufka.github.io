Privacy Policy

Idle Football Foundry · Android
Last updated: 16 October 2026

1. Developer and contact

Przemysław Chechelski, an individual developer based in Poland, publishes Idle Football Foundry (Android package: com.embGames.idleClicker). In this policy, “I” refers to the developer responsible for the personal data processing described as my own activities.

For privacy questions and requests, email p.embgames@gmail.com.

2. Progress and settings

The game stores your chosen player name, profile choices, progress, virtual balances, businesses, upgrades, daily goals, reward history and save timestamps on your device. Audio preferences are also stored locally. These records allow the game to function, preserve progress and apply bonuses. Please avoid including sensitive information in your player name.

You do not need to create an account with me to play. The game has no developer-operated player account service.

3. Google Play Games and cloud saves

The game uses Google Play Games for authentication and Saved Games. Authentication can be attempted at launch; manual connection and synchronization controls are also provided. When connected, your profile and game progress are transferred to Google's Saved Games service and associated with your Play Games player identifier, so progress can be synchronized or restored across devices. Audio preferences are not included in the cloud save. Local play remains available if authentication is not completed.

The game keeps an account association for synchronization and a local recovery copy when restoring progress. It does not receive your Google password. Google's processing is explained in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Rewarded ads and privacy choices

The game uses Google AdMob with Unity Ads through mediation to offer optional rewarded ads. Declining to watch an ad does not prevent ordinary gameplay; the optional advertising bonus is not awarded.

Advertising services may collect or receive advertising and app identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data. They use information for advertising delivery, measurement, service operation and fraud prevention. Personalized advertising depends on applicable requirements, privacy choices and service availability.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform is used to obtain privacy choices before eligible advertising requests. The game's AD SETTINGS / AD PRIVACY control provides access to available privacy options. Consent can be withdrawn where processing relies on it; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Android settings also let you reset or delete your advertising identifier. Non-personalized or limited advertising can still involve processing needed for delivery and security.

See Google's advertising privacy information and Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for provider processing and controls. Unity software used by the game may also process technical device, application and diagnostic information as described in Unity's policy.

5. Support and retention

If you email me, I process your email address, message and information you choose to provide to respond and handle your request. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. I delete completed support correspondence within 12 months after the matter is closed, unless further retention is necessary for legal obligations or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. Records kept for those exceptions are retained only as long as needed for that purpose.

Local progress remains until removed or replaced. Clearing app storage removes local data, but cloud or Android backup copies may remain. Restoring or synchronizing can bring progress back. Google's Play Games deletion instructions explain its controls for game data. Deleting cloud data does not automatically erase local copies on other devices; continued synchronization can create a new cloud save.

Google and Unity apply their own retention and deletion practices to records they independently control, as described in their privacy policies. Contact me about information I control, and use the providers' privacy channels for their independently controlled records. I will not ask for your Google password to handle a request.

6. Purposes, legal bases and recipients

Where the GDPR applies to my processing, necessary gameplay and requested synchronization are based on performance of the service you request. Processing that requires consent relies on that consent. Support handling, proportionate security and fraud prevention rely on legitimate interests in responding to requests and maintaining a reliable service, except where your rights override those interests. Compliance with a legal obligation is the basis where processing is legally required. Google's and Unity's own processing and legal bases are explained in their notices.

Relevant information is handled on your device and by Google and Unity services. Email correspondence is handled through my email provider. Service providers may process information outside your country, including outside the EEA. Their policies describe applicable transfer arrangements and safeguards. They do not necessarily act only as processors on my behalf.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law and the relevant conditions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your personal data, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent. Requests can be sent to the contact above. Proportionate information may be needed to identify the relevant data. You may complain to a competent supervisory authority, including Poland's President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

If you believe a child's information has been processed inappropriately, contact me so that the issue can be investigated and addressed with the relevant provider where necessary.
